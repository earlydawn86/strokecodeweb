Embedded Guideline Logic

  • TNK 0.25 mg/kg max 25 mg or alteplase for IVT-eligible adults within 4.5h.
  • Extended-window IVT may be reasonable at 4.5-9h or wake-up stroke with imaging mismatch/salvageable tissue.
  • ICA/M1 EVT criteria include NIHSS, mRS, ASPECTS, timing, age, and mass effect branches.
  • Basilar EVT pathway uses within 24h, NIHSS >=10, mRS 0-1, PC-ASPECTS >=6.
  • Early prevention logic is limited to acute guideline scope; long-term prevention needs dedicated guideline/local protocol.
  • TOAST etiology prediction weighs LAA, cardioembolism, lacunar SVO, other determined causes, and cryptogenic/incomplete work-up.
  • Cortical lesions increase CE/LAA likelihood; single small subcortical lesions support SVO; large or multiple subcortical lesions prompt LAA/BAD, CE, or systemic cause review.
